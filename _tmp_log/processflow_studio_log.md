## Capabilities
- Fluid: PR78 with editable kij, 3-phase V/L/aqueous flash (Michelsen multiphase RR, stability test run first, warm starts with a missing-phase check, lowest-Gibbs choice among candidate splits), PT/PH/PS/P-VF flashes, pure-component dome handling, Peneloux PR-form densities (water/MeOH/MEG shifts calibrated at 15 °C), hypos (Kesler-Lee Tc/Pc/ω, Riazi-Daubert MW, paraffin-analogue Cp).
  - v5.3: a_ij(T) and da_ij/dT are cached per (T, component set), up to 512 entries; about 25 % faster solves.
- Transport and hydrates: LBC viscosity with tabulated Vc (Vogel for water); Motiee hydrate T and margin, with ❄ flag on streams that carry water and run colder.
- Hydrate inhibition (v3): MEG added to the library; depression via Hammerschmidt (MEG) or Nielsen-Bucklin (MeOH) from the aqueous phase; dosing calculator in Analysis → Flow assurance.
- Units: valve, mixer, tee, 2/3-phase separator, expander, pump (V·ΔP with shifted volume), heater/cooler (T, duty or VF spec), air cooler (fan estimate), recycle (zero-flow start, bounded Wegstein), adjust (secant/bisection, value written back).
- Compressor: polytropic (4/8-step Richardson) or adiabatic.
- Compressor performance curves (v3): PCHIP head/efficiency vs actual flow, fan laws, surge/stonewall margins, speed-to-meet-duty, curve spec mode (outlet P solved), map chart, "generate typical curve" button.
- Anti-surge control (v4): minimum-surge-margin control line; recycle cooled back to suction state, so machine flow = max(process flow, control line). Extra power is charged; the anti-surge cooler is a separate energy stream. Fixed-P mode solves speed and recycle as a fixed point; curve mode is direct. The map shows the control line and the recycle step, and the canvas label shows "ASC x %".
- Gas scrubber (v3): Souders-Brown with mesh/vane/cyclone K (GPSA pressure correction for mesh), required/selected ID, gas load %, carry-over warning. Separators report an indicative diameter.
- Column (v3): absorber / reboiled absorber (stabiliser) / distillation (partial or total condenser). Inside-out solver: PR K and enthalpy departures in the outer loop, Newton on MESH with a 3-colour finite-difference Jacobian in the inner loop. Warm starts; specs: duty, bottoms, boil-up, reboiler T, distillate; bottoms TVP @ 37.8 °C; stage profiles. VLE only, with a free-water warning.
- Shell & tube: T, duty, min-approach or UA rating spec; heat curve and UA.
- Pipe segment: Beggs & Brill with Payne, or homogeneous; Churchill friction; Heun steps; exponential heat loss; holdup, regime, inventory, API RP 14E ratio; profile charts.
  - v5 adds private options used by SURF units: linear ambient (`T_amb_out`), heat input per metre (`q_in_W_m`, DEH), profile offsets (`z0`, `L0`), plus elevation `z` and hydrate margin `Hm` along every profile and a "Min. hydrate margin along line" result.
  - v5.1: adaptive sub-stepping inside each increment, so that no Heun step loses more than 5 % of the local pressure (minimum dL/256, with step retry). Long gas lines near their limit now march accurately instead of collapsing with "pressure falls below 0.2 bar"; ordinary cases are unchanged.
- SURF Phase 1 (v5, `procsim/surf.py`, catalogue `procsim/data/surf_catalogue.csv`, palette group "Subsea (SURF)"):
  - Well: feed at reservoir P/T; its flow is the rate. Standard-conditions flash gives gas/oil/water rates. IPR: gas back-pressure C(Pr²−Pwf²)ⁿ, PI (liquid), or Vogel. Isothermal inflow (reservoir heat shown as an energy stream). Tubing = pipe model with a geothermal linear ambient → WHP/WHT, AOF, GOR, water cut.
  - v5.3 wellhead-pressure spec: `rate_spec` = "Wellhead pressure" + `WHP`.
    - Solver (`solve_rate_for_whp`): ×/÷1.5 bracketing from the feed rate (down to about 4 %), Illinois false position on coarse tubing increments (n ≤ 6) to 0.3 bar, then Newton polish on the full increments to 0.02 bar. It returns the stable higher-rate root. Unreachable targets give clear errors. Cost: about 5–15 s per well (0.6–1.5 s per tubing evaluation).
    - Write-back: the unit sets `unit["_inlet_F"]` (in EXTRAS) and `_single_pass` writes that rate into the inlet stream. The feed's results show "Molar flow" (solved), "Specified flow" and "Rate set by".
    - `deliverability_curve` gives WHP at 0.1–2.0× the current rate (coarse increments), stopping at the first rate the well can't deliver or at AOF. It is behind a button in the well's Profile tab, with a chart (operating point, WHP target line).
  - Xmas tree + choke: catalogue tree-valve ΔP; choke outlet P / ΔP / fully open; isenthalpic; critical ratio from gas Cp/Cv (flagged as an estimate for multiphase); downstream hydrate margin (warning if < 0).
  - Template/manifold: 4/6/8 slots (overflow is an error), mix at lowest slot P (reports the spread), header friction + K loss.
  - Jumper / spool / PLET / PLEM: short pipe + K·ρv²/2 (bends × K_bend + K_extra).
  - Flowline: design presets (bare, wet insulation, PiP, flexible, bundle, buried, DEH-ready) for U and roughness (0 = catalogue), DEH W/m.
  - Riser: vertical, SCR, lazy-wave, free-hanging flexible, hybrid tower. Geometry from water depth × length factor (catenary as 2 sections; lazy-wave up → hog → sag → up, with a refusal when too short). Bøe (1981) severe-slugging screen: u_sg0 < ρ_l g α L u_sl / P0 with stratified flow and a downhill flowline → warning. v5.1: a single erosional-velocity warning with the worst ratio.
  - SSIV / HIPPS: open/closed, catalogue ΔP, HIPPS trip → no flow + warning.
  - Catalogue: parse/validate/round-trip CSV; `activate()` refreshes property-view options in place; stored in `model["surf_catalogue"]` so it is part of the solve hash and travels with the saved file.
  - Module wiring: `unitops.PROFILE_TYPES` is a literal; unitops imports surf at its end, and surf calls `register()` at its own end, so either import order works.
  - UI: HYSYS-style schematic icons, canvas labels (well label now shows WH P · T · gas rate), Profile tab for well/jumper/flowline/riser with an elevation + hydrate-margin chart.

    - Warnings: GVF outside the machine's window, boost above its maximum, power above its rating. Work energy stream with an incoming arrow; label "kW · GVF %".

- SURF Phase 3 (v5.2, `procsim/subsea_ops.py`):
  - Slugging (transient-lite): `slug_assessment(model, sol, margin)` per flowline and its downstream riser.
    - Hydrodynamic slugs at the riser base: Gregory & Scott (1969) frequency, Norris (1982) mean length (≥ 12 D), 1-in-1000 = mean × exp(3.09 × 0.5) (log-normal, Brill 1981), Gregory et al. (1978) slug-body holdup. Intermittent and B&B "Transition" count as slugging.
    - Severe slug if the riser blocks: riser fill A_r·L_r + flowline penetration x from the gas balance (P0+ΔP)(V_g−x) = P0(V_g + Q_g0(V_r+x)/Q_l) (linear in x, clamped ≥ 0), build-up time = V/Q_l.
    - The governing slug is the 1-in-1000 hydrodynamic slug, or the severe slug when Bøe flags High. Design surge = margin × governing; the UI also folds in the ramp-up sweep-out from the turndown.
  - Turndown: `turndown_envelope` recomputes flowline + riser from the solved flowline inlet over rate factors (default 0.1–1.4).
    - Outputs: arrival P/T, min hydrate margin, erosional ratio, liquid inventory (flowline + riser), Bøe/slugging, regime.
    - Limits: arrival P ≥ min, hydrate margin ≥ 0, erosional ≤ 1, no severe slugging.
    - `operating_window` gives the contiguous feasible range around 1×, the limiting constraint at each end, and the ramp-up sweep-out (inventory at min − at max, ≥ 0).
  - Field layout: `field_layout(model, sol)` builds a plan view from the topology.
    - Host at the origin; each riser's touch-down at its horizontal footprint √(L_r² − depth²); flowlines/jumpers to scale along a bearing (`model["layout"]["bearing"][riser uid]`, default 240° + 55° per riser).
    - Well slots on a ring around their template (not to scale); satellites (> 0.5 km) on their own bearing; an umbilical line.
    - Coincident units (booster at its template, SSIV at the touch-down) are offset, with templates kept fixed.
  - v5.3 cool-down: `cooldown(model, sol)` for every solved flowline, riser and heat-losing pipe.
    - Each profile point follows T(t) = T_amb + (T0 − T_amb)e^(−t/τ), with τ = C′/(U·πD).
    - C′ = A × in-situ volumetric heat capacity (B&B holdup, per-phase ρ·cp; interpolated inlet → outlet) + steel wall (default 20 mm, 7850 × 480 J/m³K) + a share (0.5) of the insulation (ρcp 1.7 MJ/m³K). Default thickness by design: PiP 40, wet/DEH 60, flexible 30, bundle 50, bare/buried 0; plain pipes 0.
    - No-touch time = τ ln((T0−Ta)/(Th−Ta)): ∞ if Th ≤ Ta or no water, 0 if already inside. Th is the inhibited Motiee temperature at the settle-out pressure (mean profile P) or the local P.
    - Uses the flowing U (conservative). Basis kept in `model["cooldown"]` (excluded from the hash). The SURF tab gains a 9th view "Cool-down" (chart at each line's critical point with dashed hydrate T and a no-touch marker, plus a table); also in the report.
  - Results on the field example: gas-condensate at 4.65 MSm³/d is stratified with Bøe ≈ 12, so no penetration and no governing slug. The window runs from 0.3× (0.1× fails on hydrate margin) up past 1.3×. Arrival P is U-shaped at very low rate (riser liquid head).
  - A low-rate oil test case (200 kmol/h, 8", 10 km downhill, 300 m SCR) gives a severe slug of about 20 m³ with about 2 h build-up.
  - Cool-down on the field example: PiP flowline no-touch about 42 h (τ about 48 h), lazy-wave flexible riser about 12 h (governing). The MEG tie-back example gives about 4.5 h; without MEG it gives 0.

## v7.8.0 — calibration to plant data, optimiser, more SURF, phase economics (2026-10-12)

- **Calibration design (my choice):** a generic estimation layer on the whole flowsheet instead of per-unit tuning (`procsim/tuning.py`, tab "🎚️ Calibrate", `ui/calibrate.py`). Data are long-table rows per *case* (operating condition): *set* rows impose the case (any unit parameter), *measure* rows give a unit result, stream property, composition or economics total with σ.
